- [humanizer-pt-br](https://github.com/mackswendhell/humanizer-pt-br), Copyright (c) 2026 mackswendhell: the Brazilian Portuguese AI writing patterns (itself based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing").

The social content skills generalize the Facebook scheduler, card generator, and Remotion studio first written for the Nota Profile project by the same author. `video-shorts` credits third-party voices and music in each video description: Piper TTS (GPL-3.0 software; the generated audio is not covered by it), the `pt_BR-faber-medium` voice (CC0 dataset, fine-tuned from a model trained on the NVIDIA Hi-Fi TTS dataset under CC BY 4.0), and the music track the project chooses. The skills ship no voice models or music files.
