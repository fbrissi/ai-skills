# INPI dispatches, deadlines, and similarity criteria

Summarized from the INPI Manual de Marcas. When a dispatch's complementary text or the current INPI rules disagree with this table, the official source prevails; say so in the issue.

## Deadline rules

- Deadlines run from the RPI publication date (the `data` attribute of `<revista>`).
- They are counted in calendar days and start on the first business day after publication. A deadline that ends on a weekend or national holiday moves to the next business day.
- A formal requirement (IPAS005) has a short deadline: **5 days** from the first business day after publication.

## Dispatch codes

|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPAS005 | Exigência formal | The filing has a formal defect. Not answering makes the application nonexistent. | Answer the requirement (service 338). | 5 days |
| IPAS395 | Exigência de pagamento | Fee missing or insufficient. | Pay the indicated GRU. | Published deadline |
| IPAS009 | Publicação para oposição | Own process: third parties may oppose. Similar mark: the owner may oppose. | Own: monitor. Similar: decide on opposition (service 332). | 60 days |
| IPAS423 | Notificação de oposição | Someone opposed the owner's application. | Optional reply (service 339); strongly recommended. | 60 days |
| IPAS136 | Exigência de mérito | The examiner requires a substantive change or document. | Comply with the requirement. | Published deadline (usually 60 days) |
| IPAS142 | Sobrestamento do exame | Examination suspended because of a prior mark or process. | Inspect the cited process; consider action against it. | No deadline; follow the cited process |
| IPAS029 | Deferimento | Application approved. Filings under service 389/394 after 20/09/2025 need no grant fee. | Check whether a grant fee applies; monitor the grant. | Published deadline when a fee applies |
| IPAS024 | Indeferimento | Application refused. | Decide on appeal. | 60 days |
| IPAS158 | Concessão | Registration granted for 10 years. | Download the certificate; schedule renewal. | Renewal in the last year of validity |
| IPAS047 / IPAS112 | Pedido considerado inexistente | Unpaid fee or unanswered formal requirement. | Investigate immediately; a new filing may be required. | — |

Unknown codes: describe the dispatch name literally, mark the action as "interpretar no pePI", and never invent a deadline.

## Similarity criteria

Assess the risk of confusion with the watched mark from the profile (name, presentation, classes, specification, business):

- **Sign:** visual, phonetic, and conceptual similarity of the dominant element. Variants of the full name, or of its distinctive combination, are high risk; marks that only share a generic or descriptive word are lower risk.
- **Market affinity:** the watched classes, and specifications close to the profile's business, increase the risk. The same name for unrelated goods is low risk.
- **Presentation:** a nominative mark covers the word in any form and weighs more than a figurative element.
- **Priority:** compare filing dates with the profile's own processes. An earlier filing by a third party threatens the owner's application; it is not only a target for opposition.

Risk levels: **alto** (recommend opposition), **médio** (monitor and reassess before the deadline), **baixo** (no action). Translate them with the rest of the issue when the profile sets another language.
